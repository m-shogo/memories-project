## Material failure: latent post-staging local lost update

- **symptom:** Source inspection of the Upload Route atomic writer at `b1be44cc` found that a target could be independently modified, replaced with another inode, changed in mode, deleted, turned into a symlink, or created after the writer's initial snapshot/alias check and before `os.replace`. The old implementation could publish over that later state. This is a source-verified risk, not an observed production overwrite.
- **evidence:** `atomic_write_bytes` compared `ROLLBACK_SNAPSHOT` before `mkstemp`, then wrote/flushed/fsynced the temporary file and called `os.replace` without a final comparison of the target's current bytes, inode or mode. Existing negatives covered prewrite drift, not post-staging drift.
- **rootCauseOrUnknown:** Verified: atomic rename and a pre-staging snapshot check do not establish local target compare-and-swap at publication.
- **failedApproach:** Treating prewrite path/byte checks, `fsync` and atomic replacement as sufficient protection against a concurrent local writer. Earlier canonical learning for deletion-load source atomic replacement (`7ff687f3`) already prohibits that assumption.
- **correction:** [`32a18898`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/32a18898c7416a4e956e51af609b71f106a71776) captures the initial target's regular-file type, bytes, inode/device and mode, then rechecks the destination and ancestor symlinks, existence, identity, mode and bytes immediately before `os.replace`.
- **recurrenceGuard:** The actual rollback validator now injects six deterministic `fsync`-stage races: byte edit, same-byte inode replacement, chmod, symlink swap to an external canary, deletion and new-file creation. Every case requires fail-closed rejection, preservation of independent state/canary and no staging residue. `scripts/validate-memory-os-entry-docs.py` binds this proof as a direct call and runs the real negative before MinIO.
- **retryCondition:** Revisit on changed Upload Route writer/rollback semantics, exact-source CI regression, or reviewed directory-fd/no-follow/locking implementation. This bounded pre-replace check does **not** eliminate a swap between the final check and `os.replace`, nor does it establish directory-fd transaction safety.

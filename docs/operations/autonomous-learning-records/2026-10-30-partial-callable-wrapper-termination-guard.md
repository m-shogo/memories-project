## Material failure — prospective CI reachability bypass

- **Symptom:** An immediately invoked `functools.partial(sys.exit, 0)()`, `partial(os.abort)()`, or a `@functools.partial(sys.exit, 0)` function/class decorator can terminate module startup or `main()` before the canonical learning guard while the guard AST remains unchanged.
- **Observed evidence:** At reviewed source `507ca5b1b67d2bf419cc0e437e4506a78a57c6d1`, the reachability visitor recognized direct exits, reflective lookups, dictionary calls, operator factories and eager lambda invocations, but it did not classify an `ast.Call` whose callable is a `partial` factory. Decorator application also executes the returned partial callable, whereas a standalone partial factory construction does not. No actual CI compromise or production incident was observed.
- **Verified root cause:** The AST reachability visitor did not model callable-wrapper invocation or partial-factory decorator application as eager execution.
- **Failed approach:** Treating only direct/reflective exit calls and operator factories as a complete pre-guard reachability proof, or treating partial construction and invocation as equivalent.
- **Correction:** [`e6cb4465`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/e6cb446562cc335f7a6cab9865c6e512b3289a51) adds a bounded lexical `partial_factory` classifier. It conservatively rejects immediate partial invocations and partial-factory decorators on function, async-function and class definitions, while leaving deferred factory creation and deferred function bodies untouched.
- **Executable recurrence guard:** Six exact-diagnostic canonical negatives cover module and main-body immediate partial termination plus function/class decorator application. Two positive controls preserve deferred partial construction and a partial invocation inside a deferred function body. This intentionally does not claim arbitrary alias closure; an eager benign partial invocation also requires review rather than silent admission.
- **Retry condition:** Reconsider a legitimate immediate partial invocation or decorator only after reviewed callable target/binding and execution-order proof, changed source preconditions, exact-source CI, paired positive/negative diagnostics, CAS and protected-authority verification. No unchanged MinIO, workflow-write, canonical-learning transport or human-approval blocker was retried.

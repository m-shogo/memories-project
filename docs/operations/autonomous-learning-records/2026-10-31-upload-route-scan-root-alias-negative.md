## Material failure A — unsafe traversal root was not rejected

- **symptom:** `current_authority_files()` rejected symlink candidates but did not inspect a scan root or its ancestors before `Path.rglob("*")`. A root-level symlink could cause traversal outside the reviewed authority tree; a non-directory root could silently produce an incomplete scan. This is a source-verified latent hazard, not a production incident.
- **evidence:** At pre-change `a3bf0bd2`, the scanner checked `root.exists()` and then immediately called `root.rglob("*")`; the previous supplemental parent-open learning explicitly identified the root-alias gap. A disposable negative now constructs a symlinked `contracts` root, a symlinked `services` ancestor of `services/import-api`, and a regular-file `contracts` root, while retaining an external canary and an ordinary positive-control authority.
- **rootCauseOrUnknown:** Verified: leaf-only alias checks do not establish traversal-root authority; `Path.rglob` can follow a linked traversal root.
- **failedApproach:** Treating candidate-level `path.is_symlink()` and file suffix filtering as sufficient for recursive authority-scope confinement.
- **correction:** [`bb16ba3f`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/bb16ba3fcef5ae2bf70ff29522c2bcfd06bf872a) checks every scan root and ancestor for symlinks before enumeration and requires an existing root to be a directory. The real disposable negative is directly invoked by the pre-MinIO validator; the entrypoint guard binds its three exact scenarios and rejects weakened no-follow/non-directory source mutations.
- **recurrenceGuard:** `prove_scan_root_alias_rejection` verifies the safe ordinary scan and rejects all three unsafe root forms without modifying an external canary. `has_upload_route_scan_root_binding` enforces ordering before `rglob` and includes two deterministic source mutations. The existing independent CI entrypoint executes the real negative before MinIO.
- **retryCondition:** Revisit when recursive root enumeration, symlink/ancestor handling, supported filesystem capabilities or CI invocation changes. This preflight is not a directory-fd transaction and does not eliminate a hostile swap after the check; do not claim complete TOCTOU prevention.

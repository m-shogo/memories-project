- **Symptom:** A CI entrypoint can preserve the canonical `main` or `validate_learning_guards` function definition while rebinding its module attribute through `sys.modules[__name__].name = ...`, `del sys.modules[__name__].name`, or `sys.modules[__name__].__dict__['name'] = ...`. These AST nodes do not require a lexical `Name(Store)` of the protected binding or a direct `globals()` call.
- **Observed evidence:** At reviewed source `c0d857ed2f962f18e257d3f2480b3795583ff1fc`, `module_scope_rebinds` recognized direct names and `globals/vars/locals` subscript stores but not direct attribute stores/deletes or module-dictionary subscript stores. The actual CI entrypoint had no observed malicious mutation; this was a prospective static gap.
- **Verified root cause:** Lexical and direct namespace-call checks do not exhaust module alias-based mutation.
- **Failed approach:** Treating `Name(Store/Del)` and `globals()` subscript checks as complete binding identity proof.

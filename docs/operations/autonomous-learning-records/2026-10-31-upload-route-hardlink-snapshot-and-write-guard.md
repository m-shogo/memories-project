## Material failure: hardlink alias escaped the pinned Upload Route reader's single-name authority check

- **symptom:** A regular authority file with two hardlink names passed `read_regular_bytes` and `read`, and could be considered a valid snapshot or atomic-write target despite an unreviewed alias to the same inode. This is a source-verified latent aliasing risk, not an observed production compromise.
- **evidence:** At parent `4c891b9c2fdbc9fcac47db473746b28ee7438483`, the pinned reader checked symlinks, regular-file type, device/inode, size, mtime and ctime, but did not require `st_nlink == 1` at preflight, pinned descriptor or final live pathname. Prior [Upload Route hardlinked source input learning](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/blob/so/docs/operations/autonomous-learning-records/2026-10-30-upload-route-hardlink-input-identity.md) already established the same hardlink failure class in the distinct Go/Python source-path guard; that proven mechanism was reused rather than repeating an unchanged failed assumption.
- **rootCauseOrUnknown:** Verified missing hardlink-count identity predicates in the reconciler's separate regular-byte reader. Whether any current checked-out authority is hardlinked is not asserted; the disposable fixture demonstrates the missing rejection.
- **failedApproach:** Treating non-symlink regular-file status and stable device/inode metadata as proof that the authority inode has only one reviewed pathname.
- **correction:** [`39979f9c`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/39979f9c4acc64ef8cf40eb1347e6fd5a1adb3a7) requires exactly one hardlink at preflight, pinned `fstat` and final live `lstat`. Because the reader is shared by snapshot, write-preflight and owned rollback checks, the fail-closed identity predicate applies to those paths without changing their production evidence or authority.
- **recurrenceGuard:** The existing CI-bound disposable Upload Route negative creates a real `os.link` alias, rejects both names through the actual byte/text readers and rejects atomic publication through the same reader, verifies no original byte mutation, unlinks the alias and confirms a restored positive read. The independent entry-document AST guard requires all three `st_nlink == 1` predicates and rejects three source mutations that weaken each predicate. The real negative remains a direct call from its existing pre-MinIO CI entrypoint.
- **retryCondition:** Revisit only after an explicitly reviewed hardlinked authority ownership model, reader/rollback/write call graph change, or new exact-source CI evidence. Do not accept a hardlink merely because `Path.resolve` and symlink checks pass; do not retry unchanged external MinIO or workflow-write permission blockers.

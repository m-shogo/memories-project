## Material failure — prospective nested generator-consumer bypass

- **Symptom:** `list(x for x in map(sys.exit, [0]))`, `next(x for x in map(sys.exit, [0]))` and `any(x for x in map(sys.exit, [0]))` can execute the mapped terminating callable before the canonical CI guard. Similarly, `list(x for x in map(exec, ["main = None"]))` can rebind the prewrite candidate's protected `main`.
- **Observed evidence:** At reviewed source `3d0b74a061bcd05a47b818635226bab99a7f9bd8`, the reachability and prewrite visitors checked direct map arguments to eager builtins and direct map iterators in loops/comprehensions. They did not connect an eager consumer to a generator expression whose inner iterator was a map call. A standalone generator expression remained correctly deferred. This is a prospective static gap, not observed CI compromise or a production incident.
- **Verified root cause:** The AST's outer builtin call receives an `ast.GeneratorExp`, not an `ast.Call` to `map`; the prior consumer guard examined only direct map arguments.
- **Failed approach:** Treating direct map arguments as complete coverage of eager builtin iteration, or treating every generator expression as eagerly consumed.
- **Correction / executable recurrence guard:** [`d62bad60`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/d62bad602689587a888cabeb87ffe5b19c3e7dd8) extends the existing bounded eager-consumer predicate to generator-expression arguments with direct map iterators. Four canonical exact-diagnostic negatives cover module and main-body nested generator consumption; two nested prewrite negatives cover mapped exec. Two deferred function-body positives preserve safe non-executing declarations.
- **Retry condition:** Reconsider a legitimate eager nested generator consumer only after reviewed callable/iterator binding and evaluation order, changed preconditions, bounded prewrite, exact-source CI, correct first diagnostic, CAS and protected-authority verification. Arbitrary iterator aliases, nested factories and all transitive consumers remain outside the proven closure.

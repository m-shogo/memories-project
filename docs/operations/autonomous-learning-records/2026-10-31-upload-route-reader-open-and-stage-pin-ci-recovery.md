## Material failure A — reader's no-follow open raced a validated regular leaf

- **symptom:** The Upload Route pinned regular-file reader checked `lstat` before `os.open(..., O_NOFOLLOW|O_NONBLOCK)`, but a symlink replacement at the open boundary raised a raw OS exception rather than the fixed `ReconcileFailure` diagnostic. This was a source-verified latent failure-class gap, not a production incident.
- **evidence:** Initial `so` HEAD `ab96f629c107955a1d90c027a15f70ecb94862a9` used an unguarded `descriptor = os.open(...)` after its regular-file preflight. A disposable test now swaps the initially valid leaf to a symlink pointing at an external byte canary at the actual `os.open` call.
- **rootCauseOrUnknown:** Verified check-to-open alias race and missing scoped `OSError` classification; actual production occurrence is unknown.
- **failedApproach:** Treating pre-open `lstat` and `O_NOFOLLOW` as sufficient to guarantee a stable domain failure type.
- **correction:** [`4c979ae8`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/4c979ae8db964aeb9e91d6e41c3233b858e1e484) converts acquisition `OSError` into a fixed `ReconcileFailure("concurrent authority drift during read")` with suppressed raw exception context; it never retries with alias-following flags.
- **recurrenceGuard:** The existing directly CI-invoked `prove_nonblocking_special_file_reads` injects the symlink at the real open call and checks the exact error class, suppressed context, restored transport, symlink identity and unchanged external canary. [`de04b651`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/de04b651115bc2a5f1d3567ee3d744d0bfa1ddd9) adds AST-bound classification and mutation negatives to the pre-MinIO entrypoint.
- **retryCondition:** Revisit only if the pinned reader, no-follow acquisition, source-bound negative, or platform error contract changes. No atomic multi-path read transaction is claimed.

## Material failure B — a failed rename could delete a newly reused stage name

- **symptom:** Earlier exact-source [Autonomous Learning Guard #41194970789](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/actions/runs/41194970789) FAILED inside `prove_failed_publication_temp_reuse_preserved` with a `FileNotFoundError`. The fixture removes the staged file at the replace syscall and immediately creates an independent same-name canary. The canary was absent at its final read. This is a disposable negative-fixture failure, not observed production deletion.
- **evidence:** The prior writer closed the staged stream before rename, retained only device/inode for failed-publication cleanup, and unlinked a stage name when that pair matched. An unlinked inode can be recycled after its last descriptor closes. The CI failure is consistent with such reuse; the exact recycled inode number was **not** logged, so the individual allocation is not asserted as directly observed.
- **rootCauseOrUnknown:** Verified unsafe ownership model: device/inode equality without a retained open descriptor does not exclude inode recycling. The exact OS allocation history of the failed CI fixture is unknown.
- **failedApproach:** Treating device/inode-only identity after closing the staging descriptor as sufficient proof that a later pathname still belongs to the writer.
- **correction:** [`5ede2d8c`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/5ede2d8c9aa6d3ec0d1f1bd58b6be4a20d5048d0) duplicates and holds the staged descriptor through publication and cleanup, preventing inode recycling while the ownership comparison is made. The original fdopen ownership guard remains intact.
- **recurrenceGuard:** The failed-publication negative now captures the pinned descriptor, requires it to remain valid after staged unlink with `st_nlink=0`, requires the independent canary to have a different inode, and verifies `EBADF` after cleanup plus unchanged original/canary bytes. [`de04b651`](https://github.com/m-shogo/memories-project/commit/de04b651115bc2a5f1d3567ee3d744d0bfa1ddd9) binds the live negative and staged descriptor/close semantics to the existing pre-MinIO CI entrypoint with deterministic source mutations.
- **retryCondition:** Revisit on changed staging, descriptor duplication, fdopen transfer, rename/cleanup, inode ownership or CI execution topology. This is **not** atomic leaf CAS; a hostile leaf replacement between stat and unlink remains outside the proof. Descriptor-duplication failure cleanup requires separate review.
